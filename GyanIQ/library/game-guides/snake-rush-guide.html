Snake Rush Guide: Rules, Dice Probability aur Tips

Game Guides (Fun Zone) · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

GyanIQ ka Snake Rush computer ke khilaaf ek board race hai. Aap dice roll karte ho, token aage badhate ho, ladder se upar chadhte ho, snake se neeche fisalte ho aur koshish karte ho ki sabse pehle square 100 par pahuncho. Ye sirf luck jaisa lagta hai, par probability practice karne ka mazedaar tareeka bhi hai, jo SSC, Railway aur Banking exams ka regular topic hai.

Game ke rules

  • Aap computer ke khilaaf khelte ho aur pehli baari aapki hoti hai.
  • Roll dabate hi dice 1 se 6 ke beech ek number dikhata hai; token utne square aage badhta hai.
  • Agar aap ladder ke neeche utre to ladder ke upar tak chadh jaoge.
  • Agar aap snake ke muh par utre to uski poonchh tak fisal jaoge.
  • Jo pehle square 100 par pahunche wo jeetta hai aur result aapke statistics mein jud jaata hai.
  • Jeetne ke liye theek 100 par pahunchna zaroori hai; agar dice 100 se aage le jaye to goti wahin rehti hai aur turn nikal jaata hai.

Har turn ke peeche dice ki probability

Ek fair dice ke 6 outcomes barabar sambhavna rakhte hain, isliye har roll independent hota hai. Pichhle rolls agle roll ko nahi badalte.

Ek roll par eventProbability
6 aana1/6
Even number aana (2, 4, 6)3/6 = 1/2
4 se bada number aana (5 ya 6)2/6 = 1/3
1 se 6 ke beech koi number aana6/6 = 1

Kaam ki aadatein

  • Kisi number ke 'due' hone ka intezaar mat karo: teen baar 1 aane ke baad bhi agla roll kisi bhi number ka barabar chance rakhta hai.
  • Move Log dekho aur samjho ki aapne aur computer ne kitne roll liye.
  • Roll karne se pehle probability guess karke khud ko test karo.
  • Chhote sessions mein khelo aur padhai ka time alag rakho.

Aur kahan seekhein

Is Library ka Probability Basics note padho jahan sample space, favourable outcomes aur formula P(E) = favourable / total samjhaya hai, phir MathRush par practice karo.

Exam Tip: Har roll independent hota hai. Lucky number ka intezaar karne ke bajay socho: is outcome ka chance kitna hai? Probability ke sawaal yahi soch test karte hain.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Snake Rush mein jeetne ke liye kya karna hota hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Sabse pehle square 100 par pahunchna. Jo player pehle 100 par pahunchta hai wahi jeetta hai.

Q2. Token ladder ke neeche utre to kya hota hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Ladder ke upar tak chadh jaata hai. Ladder token ko apne upar wale square tak le jaati hai.

Q3. Fair dice par 6 aane ki probability kitni hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) 1/6. 6 barabar outcomes mein se 1 favourable hai, isliye 1/6.

Q4. Even number aane ki probability kitni hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) 1/2. Even numbers 2, 4, 6 kul 6 mein se 3 hain, yaani 3/6 = 1/2.

Q5. Lagatar teen baar 1 aane ke baad agla roll kaisa hoga?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Phir bhi kisi bhi number ka barabar chance. Dice ke rolls independent hote hain, pichhle result agle roll ko nahi badalte.
