JEE Kinematics: Equations of Motion, Projectile, Graphs

NEET & JEE · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Kinematics mein motion ko describe kiya jaata hai, yeh nahi dekha jaata ki motion ka kaaran kya hai. Yeh JEE Physics mechanics ka pehla chapter hai aur projectile motion, relative motion aur dynamics ki neev hai. Vectors, teen equations of motion aur graph reading achhe se aa jaye to kai numericals jaldi solve ho jaate hain.

Scalar, vector aur basic pairs

Scalar mein sirf magnitude hota hai, jabki vector mein magnitude aur direction dono hote hain. Kinematics ki zyadatar galtiyan in pairs ko mix karne se hoti hain.

QuantityTypeMatlab
DistanceScalarTay kiye gaye path ki total length
DisplacementVectorStart se end tak ki sabse chhoti seedhi line
SpeedScalarDistance divided by time
VelocityVectorDisplacement divided by time

Equations of motion

Constant (uniform) acceleration a ke liye, initial velocity u, final velocity v, time t aur displacement s lekar yeh teen equations use karo.

  • v = u + at
  • s = ut + (1/2)at^2
  • v^2 = u^2 + 2as

Worked example aur free fall

Example: ek car rest se (u = 0) shuru hokar 5 s tak 2 m/s^2 se accelerate karti hai. Tab v = 0 + 2 x 5 = 10 m/s aur s = 0 + (1/2) x 2 x 25 = 25 m. Free fall mein wahi idea a = g ke saath lagta hai, jahan g lagbhag 9.8 m/s^2 neeche ki taraf hai. Rest se chhode gaye patthar ke liye v = gt aur h = (1/2)gt^2 hota hai, isliye 2 s baad uski speed 19.6 m/s aur giri hui doori 19.6 m hoti hai.

Projectile motion ki basics

Speed u aur angle theta se phenke gaye projectile ka horizontal velocity constant rehta hai aur vertical acceleration g neeche ki taraf hota hai. Dono directions ek-dusre se independent hain.

  • Time of flight: T = 2u sin(theta) / g
  • Range: R = u^2 sin(2 theta) / g
  • Maximum height: H = u^2 sin^2(theta) / (2g)
  • Given speed ke liye range 45 degree par maximum hota hai, aur theta tatha 90 - theta angles par range barabar hota hai.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Neeche mein se kaun si vector quantity hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Displacement. Displacement mein magnitude aur direction dono hote hain, isliye woh vector hai. Distance, speed aur time scalar hain.

Q2. Ek car rest se 3 m/s^2 ke acceleration se chalti hai. 4 s baad uski speed kya hogi?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) 12 m/s. v = u + at mein u = 0 rakhne par v = 3 x 4 = 12 m/s.

Q3. Given launch speed ke liye projectile ka range kis angle par maximum hota hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) 45 degree. Range sin(2 theta) ke proportional hai, jo theta = 45 degree par maximum (1) hota hai.

Q4. Velocity-time graph ka slope kya deta hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) Acceleration. v-t graph ka slope per unit time velocity ka change hai, yaani acceleration.

Q5. Ek patthar rest se chhoda gaya. g = 9.8 m/s^2 lo to 3 s baad uski speed hogi:
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) 29.4 m/s. v = gt = 9.8 x 3 = 29.4 m/s.

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Range 45 degree par maximum kyun hota hai?

Range u^2 sin(2 theta) / g hota hai. sin(2 theta) ki value tab sabse badi, yaani 1, hoti hai jab 2 theta = 90 degree, matlab theta = 45 degree.

Distance aur displacement mein kya difference hai?

Distance tay kiye gaye path ki total length hai aur kabhi negative nahi hoti. Displacement direction ke saath position ka seedha change hai; yeh distance se kam ya zero bhi ho sakta hai.
