JEE aur NEET ke liye Periodic Table Trends

NEET & JEE · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Periodic trends JEE aur NEET Chemistry ke sabse zyada marks dilane wale topics mein se hain, kyunki ek hi idea se kai sawal hal ho jate hain. Agar tum jaante ho ki nuclear charge aur shells ki sankhya kaise badalti hai, to atomic radius, ionisation energy aur electronegativity ka anuman laga sakte ho. Is note mein trend table, blocks aur famous exceptions hain.

Modern periodic law aur blocks

Modern periodic law ke anusar elements ke properties unke atomic number ka periodic function hoti hain. Table mein 18 groups aur 7 periods hain.

BlockGroupsNote
s-block1 aur 2Bahari electron s orbital mein aata hai
p-block13 se 18Noble gases isi mein hain
d-block3 se 12Transition elements
f-blockAlag rakha jata haiLanthanoids aur actinoids

Properties ke trends

Ek period mein nuclear charge badhta hai jabki shells ki sankhya wahi rehti hai. Group mein neeche jaane par naye shells judte hain.

PropertyPeriod mein left se rightGroup mein upar se neeche
Atomic radiusGhatti haiBadhti hai

ElectronegativityBadhti haiGhatti hai

Metals, non-metals, metalloids, noble gases

  • Metals table ke left aur centre mein hote hain.
  • Non-metals upar right side mein hote hain.
  • Metalloids jaise B, Si, Ge, As, Sb aur Te staircase line ke paas hote hain.
  • Noble gases (group 18) ka outer shell full hota hai, isliye woh bahut kam reactive hain.

Famous exceptions

  • N ki ionisation energy O se zyada hai, kyunki N ka 2p subshell stable half-filled hai.
  • Be ki ionisation energy B se aur Mg ki Al se zyada hai, kyunki full-filled s subshell stable hota hai.
  • Cl ki electron affinity F se zyada hai, kyunki F ke chhote 2p space mein electrons ka repulsion zyada hota hai.
  • Fluorine sabse zyada electronegative element hai.

Quick tricks

  • Period mein right jaane par radius ghatta hai, jabki IE, EN aur EA badhte hain.
  • Group mein neeche jaane par ulta hota hai.
  • Isoelectronic ions mein protons jitne zyada, size utna chhota.
Exam Tip: Pehle table mein position dekhkar trend tay karo, phir exception pairs (N-O, Be-B, Cl-F) check karo. GyanIQ ke JEE Warrior (Chemistry) mein practice karo.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Modern periodic law kis par based hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Atomic number. Moseley ne dikhaya ki periodic properties ka base atomic number hai, atomic mass nahi.

Q2. Kis element ki electronegativity sabse zyada hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Fluorine. Fluorine sabse zyada electronegative element hai.

Q3. Kiski first ionisation energy zyada hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) Nitrogen. Nitrogen ka 2p subshell half-filled hone se stable hai, isliye uski IE oxygen se zyada hai.

Q4. Period mein left se right jaane par atomic radius generally:
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) Ghatti hai. Same shell mein nuclear charge badhne se electrons paas khinchte hain, isliye radius ghatti hai.

Q5. Inmein se kaun metalloid hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Silicon. Silicon metalloid hai; sodium aur calcium metals hain aur neon noble gas hai.

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Nitrogen ki ionisation energy oxygen se zyada kyun hai?

Nitrogen ka 2p subshell half-filled hokar extra stable hai. Usse electron nikalne mein oxygen se zyada energy lagti hai.

Chlorine ki electron affinity fluorine se zyada kyun hai?

Fluorine bahut chhota hai, isliye jude hue electron par 2p ke electrons ka strong repulsion lagta hai. Chlorine ka bada 3p space yeh repulsion kam karta hai.
