Mughal Empire: Rulers, Battles aur Exam ke Key Facts

History (Itihaas) · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Mughal Empire ne 1526 se lekar 1700 ke shuruaati dashak tak Bharat ke bade hisse par raj kiya aur administration, art aur architecture par gehri chhaap chhodi. Panipat ke yudh, Akbar ke sudhar aur Shah Jahan ki imaarton se SSC, UPSC aur Railway exams mein aksar sawaal aate hain. Is note mein rulers ko kram se aur sabse zyada puchhe jaane wale facts ke saath diya gaya hai.

Rulers ek nazar mein

Chhe mahan Mughal ek ke baad ek aaye. Pehle kram yaad karo, phir har naam ke saath ek bada fact jodo.

Babur, Humayun aur Sher Shah ka antraal

  • Babur ne 1526 mein Panipat ke pehle yudh mein Ibrahim Lodi ko topon aur bandooqon ki madad se haraya aur Mughal vansh ki neev rakhi. 1527 mein usne Khanwa mein Rana Sanga ko haraya.
  • Humayun Sher Shah Suri se Chausa (1539) aur Kannauj (1540) mein haara aur use Bharat chhodna pada.
  • Sher Shah Suri ne 1540 se 1545 tak raj kiya. Usne Grand Trunk Road banwayi, chaandi ka rupaya chalaya aur land revenue vyavastha sudhari.
  • Humayun ne 1555 mein Delhi wapas jeeti, par 1556 mein uski mrityu ho gayi.

Mahan Akbar

  • Panipat ka dusra yudh (1556): Bairam Khan ki leadership mein Akbar ki sena ne Hemu ko haraya.
  • Mansabdari pratha: har officer ko ek mansab (rank) milta tha, jisse uski salary aur rakhe jaane wale sainik tay hote the.
  • Land revenue: Raja Todar Mal ki vyavastha mein zameen ki paimaish hui aur upaj aur keemton ke aadhar par tax tay hua.
  • Din-i-Ilahi (1582) naitik jeevan ka ek code tha, aur Akbar ne Sulh-i-Kul yaani sabke saath shanti ki neeti apnayi.

Jahangir, Shah Jahan aur Aurangzeb

  • Jahangir ne Agra Fort mein nyay ki zanjeer lagwayi, taaki koi bhi prajajan nyay maang sake.
  • Shah Jahan architecture ke liye jaana jaata hai: Mumtaz Mahal ki yaad mein Agra ka Taj Mahal, Delhi ka Red Fort aur Jama Masjid.
  • Baad ke Mughalon mein Aurangzeb ne sabse lamba raj kiya aur uske samay empire sabse bada hua. 1707 mein uski mrityu ke baad empire kamzor padne laga.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Panipat ka pehla yudh (1526) Babur aur kiske beech hua?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) Ibrahim Lodi. Babur ne 1526 mein Panipat mein Delhi ke Sultan Ibrahim Lodi ko haraakar Mughal raj shuru kiya.

Q2. 1539 mein Chausa ke yudh mein Humayun ko kisne haraya?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Sher Shah Suri. Sher Shah Suri ne Chausa mein aur phir 1540 mein Kannauj mein Humayun ko haraya.

Q3. 1582 mein Din-i-Ilahi kisne shuru kiya?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Akbar. Akbar ne apni sahishnu neeti ke tahat Din-i-Ilahi naam ka naitik code shuru kiya.

Q4. Agra ka Taj Mahal kis Mughal ruler ne banwaya?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) Shah Jahan. Shah Jahan ne apni patni Mumtaz Mahal ki yaad mein Taj Mahal banwaya.

Q5. Raja Todar Mal kis Mughal sudhar se jude hain?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Land revenue vyavastha. Akbar ke mantri Todar Mal ne zameen ki paimaish aur revenue vyavastha ko sangathit kiya.
