Blood Relations in Reasoning: Terms, Symbols and Tricks

Reasoning & Puzzles · 3 min read · Updated: October 2026

Blood relation questions give a short chain of statements such as A is the father of B and ask how two people are related. They appear regularly in the reasoning section of SSC, Railway, Police and Banking exams. Once you know the relationship terms and follow one fixed drawing method, these questions become quick and safe marks.

Core relationship terms

StatementRelation
Father's fatherPaternal grandfather
Mother's fatherMaternal grandfather
Father's brotherPaternal uncle (chacha or tau)
Mother's brotherMaternal uncle (mama)
Father's sisterPaternal aunt (bua)
Mother's sisterMaternal aunt (mausi)
Sister's husbandBrother-in-law
Wife's brotherBrother-in-law
Brother's wifeSister-in-law
Son's wifeDaughter-in-law
Husband's fatherFather-in-law
Uncle's son or daughterCousin

The symbols method

Turn every person into a letter and every link into a sign, so you never hold the chain in your head.

  • Use a square or the sign + for a male and a circle or the sign - for a female.
  • A horizontal line between two people shows husband and wife or brother and sister.
  • A vertical line going down shows parent to child, so older generations stay higher on the page.
  • Draw the statements in the given order, one at a time, and never skip a person.

Family tree with solved examples

Example 1: Pointing to a man, a woman says, He is the son of my mother's only brother. Her mother's brother is her maternal uncle, and his son is her cousin, so the man is her cousin brother. Example 2: A is the brother of B, B is the sister of C, and C is the father of D. A, B and C are siblings, A is male and C is D's father, so A is D's paternal uncle.

Common traps

  • Gender not given: A is the child of B does not tell you if A is a son or a daughter, so choose cannot be determined if it is an option.
  • Father's side and mother's side are different: father's brother and mother's brother are not the same relation.
  • Only son means no other son, but there can be daughters.
  • My father's son means brother, or the speaker himself if the speaker is male; read the exact words.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I solve blood relation questions quickly?

Learn the key relationship terms, draw each statement as a small tree with male and female symbols, then read the link between the two people asked. Daily practice of 10 to 15 questions builds speed.

What should I do when gender is not given in a blood relation question?

Do not assume the gender. If cannot be determined is an option, choose it unless a later statement reveals the gender.
