Percentage Tricks: Fraction Table, Formulas and Shortcuts

Maths · 3 min read · Updated: October 2026

Percentage means 'out of 100' and is the base of profit and loss, discount, interest, data interpretation and many other chapters. Speed here saves minutes in SSC, Railway, Banking and Police exams. Learn the fraction table by heart, then use the formulas and shortcuts below to solve most questions in a few seconds.

What is percentage

Percent comes from per centum, meaning per hundred. So x% is x/100. To find x% of a number, multiply the number by x/100. To find what percent A is of B, use (A divided by B) multiplied by 100.

Increase, decrease and successive change

Percentage change = (change divided by original value) multiplied by 100. For two successive changes of a% and b%, the net change is a + b + (ab/100) percent. Put a minus sign for a decrease.

  • 20% increase then 10% increase: 20 + 10 + 2 = 32% net increase.
  • 20% decrease twice: -20 - 20 + 4 = -36%, a 36% net decrease.
  • If a value rises by r%, to return to the original it must fall by r/(100 + r) x 100 percent. A 25% rise needs a 20% fall.

Shortcut tricks

  • 10%: just move the decimal point one place left.
  • 5% is half of 10%; 15% is 10% plus 5%.
  • 25% means divide by 4; 50% means divide by 2.
  • x% of y equals y% of x, so 4% of 50 is easy: it is 50% of 4, which is 2.

Worked examples

  • Price: a shirt costing 200 rupees rises by 20%. New price = 200 x 1.2 = 240 rupees.
  • Population: a town of 50,000 grows 10% each year for 2 years. Net growth = 10 + 10 + 1 = 21%, so population = 50,000 x 1.21 = 60,500.
  • A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. Net change = 10 - 10 - 1 = -1%, a 1% loss.

Practice Quiz

Q1. What is 1/8 expressed as a percentage?
Show answer

Correct answer: B) 12.5%. 1/8 = 0.125, which is 12.5%.

Q2. A price rises by 20% and then by 10%. What is the net increase?
Show answer

Correct answer: C) 32%. Net change = 20 + 10 + (20 x 10)/100 = 32%.

Q3. What is 25% of 480?
Show answer

Correct answer: C) 120. 25% is one fourth, and 480 divided by 4 is 120.

Q4. A value falls by 20% and then falls by another 20%. What is the net decrease?
Show answer

Correct answer: B) 36%. Net = -20 - 20 + 4 = -36%, so a 36% decrease.

Q5. A town has 50,000 people and grows 10% in one year. What is the new population?
Show answer

Correct answer: C) 55,000. 10% of 50,000 is 5,000, so the population becomes 55,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the formula for successive percentage change?

For two changes of a% and b%, net change = a + b + (ab/100) percent. Use a negative sign for each decrease.

How do I calculate percentage increase quickly?

Divide the increase by the original value and multiply by 100. For speed, use fractions: a rise from 80 to 100 is 20 on 80, which is 1/4, or 25%.
