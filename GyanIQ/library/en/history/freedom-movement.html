Indian Freedom Movement: Gandhian Era Movements and Timeline

History · 3 min read · Updated: October 2026

India's freedom struggle was a long journey from the founding of the Indian National Congress in 1885 to Independence in 1947. After 1917 it came under the leadership of Mahatma Gandhi, who used truth and non-violence. Dates and places of these events are asked in almost every SSC, UPSC, Railway and Police history paper, so a clear timeline helps.

Before Gandhi: two turning points

  • 1885: The Indian National Congress was founded, with A. O. Hume playing a key role. Its first session was held in Bombay.
  • 1905: Lord Curzon divided Bengal. This led to the Swadeshi movement and boycott of foreign goods.

Gandhian movements in short

  • Champaran 1917: Gandhi's first satyagraha in India, in support of indigo farmers of Bihar.
  • Non-Cooperation 1920-22: people boycotted schools, courts and foreign cloth. It was stopped after violence at Chauri Chaura in 1922.
  • Civil Disobedience 1930: Gandhi walked from Sabarmati to Dandi to break the salt law.
  • Quit India 1942: launched on 8 August with the slogan Do or Die.

Towards freedom

After the Quit India Movement, the British position in India became weaker, and talks on transfer of power began. India became independent on 15 August 1947, along with the Partition of the country.

Practice Quiz

Q1. In which year was the Indian National Congress founded?
Show answer

Correct answer: B) 1885. The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885, and its first session was held in Bombay.

Q2. Gandhi's first satyagraha in India was held at
Show answer

Correct answer: C) Champaran. The Champaran Satyagraha of 1917 in Bihar, for indigo farmers, was Gandhi's first satyagraha in India.

Q3. Why was the Non-Cooperation Movement withdrawn in 1922?
Show answer

Correct answer: A) Violence at Chauri Chaura. A mob burned a police station at Chauri Chaura in February 1922, so Gandhi stopped the movement.

Q4. The Dandi March of 1930 was launched to break which law?
Show answer

Correct answer: B) Salt law. Gandhi marched from Sabarmati to Dandi to break the salt law, starting the Civil Disobedience Movement.

Q5. Which slogan was given during the Quit India Movement of 1942?
Show answer

Correct answer: D) Do or Die. On 8 August 1942 Gandhi gave the call Do or Die at Bombay, launching the Quit India Movement.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did the Jallianwala Bagh massacre happen?

It happened on 13 April 1919 at Amritsar in Punjab. British troops under General Dyer fired on an unarmed crowd gathered in the garden.

Which movement began with the Dandi March?

The Civil Disobedience Movement began with the Dandi March in 1930. Gandhi and his followers broke the salt law on the sea coast.
