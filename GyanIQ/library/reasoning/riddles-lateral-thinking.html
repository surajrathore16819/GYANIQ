Riddles aur Lateral Thinking: Patterns, Examples aur Tricks

Reasoning & Paheliyan · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Riddle ek chhota sawal hota hai jo kisi cheez ko ghuma-phira kar describe karta hai aur uska naam poochhta hai. Riddles solve karne se lateral thinking banti hai, yaani problem ko naye angle se dekhne ki aadat. Ye aadat competitive exams ke reasoning, puzzle aur verbal aptitude sawalon mein kaam aati hai.

Riddles ke common patterns

Riddle ko clues mein todein

Turant answer ka guess na lagayein. Chhote steps mein kaam karein.

  • Riddle ke har noun, verb aur number ke neeche line khinchein.
  • Ajeeb ya extra lagne wale words par dhyan dein, kyunki wahi aksar asli clue hote hain.
  • Do-teen guess lagayein aur har guess ko saare clues se check karein.
  • Atak jaayein to viewpoint badlein: ulta sochein, chhote ya bade scale par dekhein, ya pun dhoondhein.

Chhe original examples answers ke saath

  • Main envelope ke ek corner mein baitha rehta hoon, phir bhi kai shehar ghoomta hoon. Answer: postage stamp, kyunki wo corner mein rehta hai aur letter ke saath safar karta hai.
  • Dhoop mein main tumhare peeche chalta hoon, andhere mein gaayab ho jaata hoon aur bina awaaz tumhari har harkat copy karta hoon. Answer: parchhai (shadow).
  • Ek shelf par 12 books hain aur aap 5 ko chhodkar baaki sab nikaal lete hain. Kitni bachi? Answer: 5, kyunki 5 ko chhodkar sab nikaalne par 5 hi bachti hain.
  • 1 kg cotton bhaari hai ya 1 kg iron? Answer: dono barabar hain, kyunki dono 1 kg hain.
  • Mera head hai, tail hai, par body nahi hai. Answer: coin (sikka), jiske dono sides head aur tail kehlate hain.
  • 10th floor par rehne wala aadmi dhoop mein lift se 6th floor tak jaakar paidal chadhta hai, par baarish mein seedha 10th tak jaata hai. Answer: wo chhote kad ka hai, sirf button 6 chhu paata hai aur baarish mein umbrella se button 10 daba leta hai.

Exams mein riddles ka fayda

Riddles dhyan se padhna, assumptions par sawal uthana aur ek se zyada idea test karna sikhati hain. Ye skills seating arrangement, coding-decoding aur logical puzzles mein kaam aati hain. Roz das minute riddles dimaag ko flexible rakhti hain, aur aap GyanIQ ke Socho-Zara game par aur practice kar sakte hain.

Exam Tip: Jab answer bahut easy ya bahut mushkil lage, to calculation se pehle sawal dobara padhein aur literal matlab ya chhupa hua word dhoondhein.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Main dhoop mein tumhare peeche chalta hoon aur andhere mein gaayab ho jaata hoon. Main kaun hoon?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) Shadow. Shadow roshni mein banti hai aur aapki harkat copy karti hai, par andhere mein nahi dikhti.

Q2. Shelf par 12 books hain aur aap 5 ko chhodkar baaki sab nikaal lete hain. Kitni books bachin?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) 5. 5 ko chhodkar sab nikaalne ka matlab hai ki 5 books bachi rehti hain.

Q3. 1 kg cotton aur 1 kg iron mein kaun bhaari hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Dono barabar. Dono ka weight 1 kg hai, isliye barabar hain; cotton bas zyada jagah gherti hai.

Q4. Riddle solve karne ka achha pehla step kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Clues aur keywords ke neeche line khinchna. Noun, verb aur numbers par mark karne se asli clue pakad mein aata hai.

Q5. Lateral thinking ka main matlab kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) Naye angle se sochna. Lateral thinking mein problem ko obvious raste ki jagah kisi doosre side se dekha jaata hai.
