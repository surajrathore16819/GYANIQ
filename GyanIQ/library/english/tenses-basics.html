English Tenses Basics: Formulas, Signal Words aur Common Traps

English · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Tense batata hai ki action kab hota hai: present, past ya future mein. SSC, Railway, Banking aur Police exams mein fill in the blanks, error spotting aur sentence improvement ke sawal sahi verb form chunne par tike hote hain. Har tense ka formula aur kuch signal words aate hon to aise kai sawal seconds mein solve ho jaate hain.

Tense formulas examples ke saath

TenseFormulaExample
Simple presentV1 (he, she, it ke saath s/es)She plays chess.
Present continuousis/am/are + V-ingHe is reading now.
Present perfecthas/have + V3They have finished.
Simple pastV2I watched a film.
Past continuouswas/were + V-ingWe were walking.
Past perfecthad + V3The train had left.
Simple futurewill + V1I will call you.
Future continuouswill be + V-ingShe will be sleeping.
Future perfectwill have + V3He will have reached.

Subject-verb agreement basics

  • he, she, it aur singular noun ke saath present verb mein s ya es lagta hai: she writes, the bus stops.
  • I, you, we, they aur plural noun ke saath base verb aata hai: they play, we write.
  • s, sh, ch, x ya o par khatam verbs mein es lagta hai (watches, goes). Consonant + y wale verb mein y badalkar ies ho jaata hai (study se studies).
  • Singular ke saath is, plural ke saath are, I ke saath am; singular ke saath has, plural ke saath have; singular ke saath does, plural ke saath do.

Signal words

  • Since (point in time) aur for (period) aam taur par present perfect ke saath aate hain: I have lived here since 2010, for ten years.
  • Yesterday, ago, last week aur in 1999 simple past ki taraf ishara karte hain.
  • Already, just, yet aur ever present perfect ka sanket dete hain.
  • Tomorrow, next week aur soon future ka, tatha now aur at the moment continuous form ka sanket dete hain.

Common traps

  • did, does, do aur did not ke baad hamesha base verb (V1) aata hai: He did not go, 'did not went' nahi.
  • know, like, want aur believe jaise verbs aam taur par continuous mein nahi aate: 'I know' bolein, 'I am knowing' nahi.
  • Each, every, either, neither aur everyone ke saath singular verb aata hai.
  • Future ke matlab wale if ya when clause mein present tense aata hai: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.

Practice Quiz

Q1. She ___ to school every day.
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: A) goes. Subject she singular hai aur sentence aadat batata hai, isliye simple present form goes sahi hai.

Q2. I have lived in this city ___ 2015.
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) since. Present perfect mein point in time (2015) ke saath since aata hai.

Q3. The train ___ already left when we reached the station.
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) had. Do past actions mein pehle hui action past perfect (had left) mein aati hai.

Q4. He did not ___ the answer.
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) know. did not ke baad base verb (V1) aata hai.

Q5. Look! It ___ raining.
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) is. 'Look!' abhi ho rahi action dikhata hai, isliye present continuous hai raining mein is chahiye.

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

since aur for mein kya difference hai?

Since ke saath starting point aata hai, jaise 2010 ya Monday. For ke saath time ki length aati hai, jaise five years ya two hours.

Verb mein s ya es kaise lagayein?

Zyadatar verbs mein s lagayein (plays). s, sh, ch, x ya o par khatam verbs mein es lagayein (watches, goes). Consonant + y par khatam verb mein y ko ies kar dein (studies).
