Bharatiya Samvidhan ki Prastavana: Keywords aur Objectives

Polity & Samvidhan · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Prastavana (Preamble) Bharatiya Samvidhan ka chhota sa parichay hai. Ye batati hai ki Samvidhan kisne banaya, Bharat kaisa State hai aur uske goals kya hain. Iski shuruaat We, the People of India shabdon se hoti hai. Iske keywords, amendment aur dates par SSC, UPSC, Railway aur State exams mein baar-baar sawal puchhe jaate hain.

Prastavana kya kehti hai

Prastavana declare karti hai ki Bharat ke logon ne Bharat ko ek Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic banane ka sankalp liya hai. Iske vichar Jawaharlal Nehru dwara 1946 mein rakhe gaye Objectives Resolution se aaye hain. Saari shakti ka source janta hai, isiliye ye We, the People se shuru hoti hai.

State ke swaroop ke paanch keywords

  • Sovereign: Bharat andar aur bahar ke maamlon mein kisi bahari control se mukt hai.
  • Socialist: State inequality ghatane aur social aur economic justice dene ka goal rakhta hai.
  • Secular: State ka koi official dharm nahi hai aur wo sabhi dharmon ko barabar manta hai.
  • Democratic: Satta janta ke haath mein hai, jo apne shasak chunti hai.
  • Republic: State ka head President elected hota hai, hereditary nahi.

Chaar Objectives

ObjectivePrastavana mein arth
Justice (Nyay)Social, economic aur political
Liberty (Swatantrata)Vichar, abhivyakti, vishwas, dharm aur upasana ki
Equality (Samta)Pratishtha aur avsar ki

Dates aur 42nd Amendment

Constituent Assembly ne Samvidhan 26 November 1949 ko adopt kiya aur ye 26 January 1950 se lagu hua. Original Prastavana mein Bharat ko Sovereign Democratic Republic kaha gaya tha. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976 ne Socialist aur Secular words jode aur nation ki unity ke baad integrity bhi joda. Prastavana mein ab tak yahi ek amendment hua hai.

Legal Position

  • Prastavana ko court mein enforce nahi kiya ja sakta; sirf iske aadhar par koi case nahi hota.
  • Ye na Parliament ki power ka source hai aur na us par koi limit.
  • Berubari Union case (1960) mein Supreme Court ne kaha ki ye Samvidhan ka part nahi hai.
  • Kesavananda Bharati case (1973) mein Court ne ise Samvidhan ka part maana aur interpretation ka guide bataya.
Exam Tip: SSSDR yaad rakhiye: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic, aur 26 November 1949 ko Constitution Day jaaniye. GyanIQ ke Quiz Game par khud ko test kijiye.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Prastavana mein Socialist aur Secular words kis Amendment se jode gaye?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) 42nd Amendment. 1976 ke 42nd Amendment Act ne Socialist, Secular aur integrity words jode.

Q2. Constituent Assembly ne Samvidhan kis date ko adopt kiya?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) 26 November 1949. Ise 26 November 1949 ko adopt kiya gaya aur 26 January 1950 se lagu kiya gaya.

Q3. Prastavana ki shuruaat kin shabdon se hoti hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) We, the People of India. Prastavana We, the People of India se shuru hoti hai, jo janta ko authority ka source dikhata hai.

Q4. Kis case mein Supreme Court ne Prastavana ko Samvidhan ka part maana?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: D) Kesavananda Bharati case. Kesavananda Bharati (1973) mein Court ne Berubari ke view ko badal kar Prastavana ko Samvidhan ka part maana.

Q5. Inmein se kaun sa word 1950 ki original Prastavana mein nahi tha?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Secular. Secular word 1976 mein joda gaya; baaki teen original text mein the.
