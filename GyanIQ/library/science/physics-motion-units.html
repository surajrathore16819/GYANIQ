Motion, SI Units aur Newton ke Niyam: Basic Physics Notes

General Science · Padhne mein 3 min · Updated: October 2026

Physics ki shuruaat units aur motion se hoti hai. SSC, Railway, Police, NEET aur school exams mein SI units, speed aur velocity ka fark, equations of motion aur Newton ke niyamon se seedhe sawal aate hain. In notes mein formulas, table aur ek solved example ek hi jagah diya gaya hai.

Speed, velocity aur acceleration

  • Speed = total distance / time. Ye scalar hai, sirf magnitude hota hai
  • Velocity = displacement / time. Ye vector hai, direction bhi hoti hai
  • Acceleration = velocity mein badlav / time. Velocity ghate to ise retardation kehte hain

Motion ke teen equations

Jab acceleration uniform ho tab inka use hota hai. Yahan u initial velocity, v final velocity, a acceleration, t time aur s displacement hai.

  • Pehla: v = u + at
  • Doosra: s = ut + (1/2)at^2
  • Teesra: v^2 = u^2 + 2as

Newton ke motion ke teen niyam

  • Pehla niyam (law of inertia): jab tak bahari force na lage, vastu rest ya uniform motion mein rehti hai
  • Doosra niyam: force = mass x acceleration, yaani F = ma
  • Teesra niyam: har action ka barabar aur ulta reaction hota hai

Solved example aur weight vs mass

Example: ek car rest se shuru hokar 2 m/s^2 acceleration se 5 second chalti hai. Tab v = 0 + 2 x 5 = 10 m/s aur s = 0 + (1/2) x 2 x 25 = 25 m. Weight W = mg hota hai, jahan g lagbhag 9.8 m/s^2 hai. 60 kg mass ka weight Earth par 588 N hoga.

Practice Quiz

Q1. Force ki SI unit kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Newton. Force ki SI unit newton (N) hai, jahan 1 N = 1 kg m/s^2.

Q2. Rest se chalti vastu ka acceleration 3 m/s^2 hai. 4 second baad uski velocity kitni hogi?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) 12 m/s. v = u + at = 0 + 3 x 4 = 12 m/s.

Q3. Newton ka pehla motion law kis naam se bhi jaana jaata hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: B) Law of inertia. Pehla niyam batata hai ki vastu apni avastha badalne ka virodh karti hai, isliye ise law of inertia kehte hain.

Q4. Earth par 10 kg vastu ka weight lagbhag kitna hoga (g = 9.8 m/s^2)?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) 98 N. W = mg = 10 x 9.8 = 98 N.

Q5. Inme se kaunsi vector quantity hai?
Answer dekho

Sahi jawab: C) Velocity. Velocity mein magnitude aur direction dono hote hain, isliye ye vector hai. Baaki teen scalar hain.

Speed aur velocity mein kya fark hai?

Speed sirf batati hai ki vastu kitni tez chal rahi hai aur ye scalar hai. Velocity mein direction bhi hoti hai aur ye vector hai.

Moon par weight kam kyun hota hai?

Moon ka gravitational acceleration Earth ka lagbhag chhathaa hissa hai, isliye weight kam hota hai. Mass wahi rehta hai.
